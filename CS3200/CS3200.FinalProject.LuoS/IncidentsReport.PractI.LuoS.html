Trend by Year

The visualization analayze 17 year from 2008 to 2024 showing the trend for airline safety evlotion. It start with 608 in 2008, but in the next year the incidents raise dramatically to 1081 incidents in 2009 and then maintain this level between 1067 - 1171 incidents annually through 2022 with average of 1119.1 incidents per year. In 2023 there is a peak occur with 1176 incidents. And at the very end of the trend, there is a sharp decline to 591 incidents in 2024 which create a situation that the beginning and ending years are having a significantly lower incidents acitivities compare to the middle of the trend.
